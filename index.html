PAR Moderation

Official Community Staff Team — Roblox
We are the dedicated moderation team of Philippine Army Roleplay (PAR), a Roblox game community. Our mission is to maintain a safe, fair, and enjoyable environment for all members through consistent enforcement of community standards.
Roblox PAR Group PAR Moderation Group PAR Mod Discord PAR Main Server
Important — Moderator Scope

PAR Moderators are not obligated to perform in-game character roleplay. Our role is strictly community moderation — enforcing rules, handling reports, processing appeals, and maintaining server standards. We are community staff, not in-game participants.

What We Do

Enforce Rules

We actively monitor Discord and in-game activity to enforce the community's rules and protect members from harassment, abuse, and harmful behavior.

Handle Reports

Members can report rule violations through our ticket system. Our team reviews every report fairly and takes appropriate action.

Process Appeals

Banned members may appeal through our PAR Mod Discord server. We review all eligible appeals and ensure every case is handled with fairness.

Community Safety

Our team works around the clock to maintain a positive, inclusive space for all Philippine Army Roleplay community members.
